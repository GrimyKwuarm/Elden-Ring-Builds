Fresh save mage route

Become a gravity-slinging, sword-slicing night mage without needing a perfect run.

Start as Astrologer, grab the Meteorite Staff and Rock Sling early, add Carian Slicer for close fights, then graduate into Night Comet and boss-melting setup spells.

Start the route

Build identity

Fast spells, big staggers, sneaky nukes.

Starting class

Astrologer is the smoothest start: high Intelligence, early staff, and enough Mind to cast comfortably.

Main stats

Vigor first, then Intelligence and Mind. Aim for 25 Vigor before you get greedy with damage.

Core loop

Open with Rock Sling for stance pressure, punish close enemies with Carian Slicer, and use Night Comet when enemies dodge normal casts.

Step by step

The route

Start Astrologer and set your early rules

Choose Astrologer, take Golden Seed as your keepsake, and keep the Short Sword as a backup only. Spend early levels on Vigor until survival feels stable, then push Intelligence and Mind.

  • Target early stats: 20 Vigor, 18 Intelligence, 15 Mind.
  • Keep distance, cast Glintstone Pebble, and save flasks for mistakes instead of trading hits.
Astrologer wiki Glintstone Pebble wiki

Unlock Torrent and the basic toolkit

Rest at Gatefront to meet Melina and get Torrent. Collect the map fragment at Gatefront Ruins, then gather nearby Golden Seeds and Sacred Tears before fighting anything dramatic.

  • Goal: 4 or more flasks before the Caelid item sprint.
  • Buy a crafting kit if you want sleep pots later, but this route does not require them.
Gatefront Ruins wiki Physick flask wiki

Meet Sellen at Waypoint Ruins

Clear the Waypoint Ruins cellar boss and unlock Sorceress Sellen. She becomes your early spell shop and later accepts the Royal House Scroll for Carian Slicer.

  • Use Pebble at range; do not stand in poison flowers.
  • After unlocking Sellen, buy any cheap spell that fits your style, but save runes for Carian Slicer.
Sellen wiki Waypoint Ruins wiki

Steal the Meteorite Staff and Rock Sling

Take the Dragon-Burnt Ruins trap chest to Sellia Crystal Tunnel, escape outside, then ride southwest to Street of Sages Ruins. Grab Meteorite Staff from the ruined building and Rock Sling from the nearby cellar.

  • Do this as a sprint, not a fight. Caelid hits hard early.
  • Meteorite Staff cannot be upgraded, but its early scaling is absurdly useful.
  • Rock Sling deals physical damage and staggers bosses beautifully.
Meteorite Staff wiki Rock Sling wiki Marked map

Pick up Royal House Scroll and buy Carian Slicer

Find the Royal House Scroll in southern Limgrave, give it to Sellen, then buy Carian Slicer. This turns the build from "wizard with a pebble" into a close-range blender.

  • Use Slicer on wolves, miners, soldiers, and anything that keeps closing distance.
  • Keep Rock Sling for shielded enemies, large enemies, and bosses.
Royal House Scroll wiki Carian Slicer wiki

Beat Margit and Godrick when you feel stable

Rock Sling makes both fights friendlier because it can break stance from a safe range. Summons are completely fine; this build is about having fun, not auditioning for a no-hit montage.

  • Recommended before Stormveil: around 25 Vigor and 25 Intelligence.
  • After Godrick, move through Liurnia toward Raya Lucaria.
Margit wiki Godrick wiki

Sneak the Academy Glintstone Key

Ride to the island west of Raya Lucaria and loot the key behind Glintstone Dragon Smarag. You can grab it without fighting the dragon if you stay calm and keep moving.

  • Enter the Academy through South Raya Lucaria Gate.
  • Mark nearby Sites of Grace so you can recover easily if the lake gets messy.
Academy key wiki Marked map

Collect Academy caster talismans

After reaching the Debate Parlor area, grab Radagon Icon for faster casting and Graven-School Talisman for more sorcery damage. These stay useful for a long time.

  • Radagon Icon is in a chest above the Debate Parlor route.
  • Graven-School Talisman is behind the Academy illusory-wall route.
Radagon Icon wiki Graven-School Talisman wiki

Defeat Rennala and respec cleanly

Rennala resists magic, so lean on Rock Sling, Carian Slicer, and summons if needed. Afterward, respec if your stats wandered.

  • Comfortable midgame target: 30 Vigor, 30 to 40 Intelligence, 20 Mind.
  • Keep enough Endurance only for your preferred armor and talismans.
Rennala wiki Respec wiki

Turn into a Sellian night mage

Return to Caelid and solve Sellia, Town of Sorcery by lighting the towers. Loot Night Comet and Staff of Loss. Staff of Loss boosts invisibility sorceries, and Night Comet is excellent because many enemies do not dodge it like normal projectiles.

  • Use Meteorite Staff for gravity spells and Staff of Loss for Night Comet once upgraded.
  • Carry both if your equip load allows it.
Night Comet wiki Staff of Loss wiki Sellia wiki Marked map

Add Magic-Shrouding Cracked Tear

Defeat the Erdtree Avatar at the northeastern Liurnia Minor Erdtree to get a Physick tear that boosts magic damage. Drink it before boss openings, then cast your highest-value spells.

  • Pair it with a defensive tear while learning fights.
  • Once confident, swap to more damage or FP support.
Magic-Shrouding Tear wiki

Optional: unlock Terra Magica

Clear Academy Crystal Cave west of Raya Lucaria, beat the Crystalians, ride the elevator up, and loot Terra Magica. It creates a magic-damage zone for boss setups and big openings.

  • Bring a strike weapon or use spells carefully against Crystalians.
  • Best use: pre-cast before a boss crosses the arena or after a stance break.
Terra Magica wiki Academy Crystal Cave wiki

Optional: build the giant laser button

When you reach Mt. Gelmir, find Primeval Sorcerer Azur for Comet Azur and defeat the Mt. Gelmir Ulcerated Tree Spirit for Cerulean Hidden Tear. This is the classic "stand in Terra Magica and delete a health bar" setup.

  • This is optional and later than the core build.
  • Use Night Comet and Rock Sling for regular play; save the laser for openings that deserve theater.
Comet Azur wiki Cerulean Hidden Tear wiki Marked map

Flask sweep checklist

  • Sacred Tears: Third Church, Callu Baptismal, Pilgrimage, Fourth Church, Irith, Bellum, Inhibition, Plague, Second Church, Stormcaller, Repose, First Church.
  • Golden Seeds: keepsake, Stormhill Shack, Fort Haight, Mistwood, Stormveil, Academy Gate Town, Main Academy Gate, Liurnia ravine, Sellia, Bestial Sanctum, Altus Highway, Outer Wall, Erdtree-Gazing Hill, Seethewater, Gelmir camp.

Farm ladder

  • Levels 10-25: Gatefront practice or Lenne's Rise boulder if you want safety.
  • Levels 25-55: Vulgar Militia in Dragonbarrow, plus Greyoll as a one-time boost.
  • Levels 55+: Liurnia/Altus dungeons, then Palace Approach later if you naturally unlock Mohgwyn.

Detour priority

  • Survival first: Weeping churches before Stormveil.
  • Power first: Meteorite Staff, Rock Sling, Carian Slicer.
  • Style first: Academy Crystal Cave for Terra Magica, Gael Tunnel for Moonveil, Sellia for Night Comet.

Level plan

Level-by-level stat plan

Astrologer starts at level 6 with 9 Vigor, 15 Mind, 9 Endurance, 8 Strength, 12 Dexterity, 16 Intelligence, 7 Faith, and 9 Arcane. This plan favors comfort first, then damage.

If you choose Moonveil

Raise Strength to 12 and Dexterity to 18, usually after level 45. Take those points from Intelligence temporarily, then respec after Rennala if it feels messy.

If you keep pure caster

Leave Strength and Dexterity alone. Put spare levels into Vigor until 40, Intelligence until 60, then Mind and Intelligence depending on whether you need casts or damage.

Final feel

Suggested loadout

Weapons

Meteorite Staff for Rock Sling. Staff of Loss for Night Comet once upgraded. Keep a light melee backup if you like, but Carian Slicer usually covers that job.

Spells

Rock Sling, Carian Slicer, Night Comet, Glintstone Pebble or Great Glintstone Shard, Terra Magica, and Comet Azur if you take the late route.

Talismans

Radagon Icon, Graven-School Talisman, Green Turtle Talisman for comfort, and any defensive talisman while learning bosses.

Physick

Magic-Shrouding Cracked Tear plus a defensive tear for exploration. Swap in Cerulean Hidden Tear when using Comet Azur setups.
